# Towera

## 1. Project Description
Towera collects, verifies, structures, and licenses high-quality Nigerian language datasets that power the next generation of AI. The company serves as the data infrastructure layer for Nigeria's 500+ languages, enabling AI companies (OpenAI, Google, Microsoft), universities, and Nigerian startups to build language models with trusted, native-speaker-verified datasets.

**Product Ecosystem:**
- **Towera Atlas** — Interactive map of every Nigerian language (speakers, dialects, states, writing systems, dataset availability)
- **Towera Voice** — Speech datasets with thousands of hours of native-speaker recordings across Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Tiv, Kanuri, Fulfulde, Ijaw, Edo, Ibibio, Nupe, and more
- **Towera Corpus** — Large text datasets: Nigerian novels, proverbs, conversations, Nollywood scripts, news, government documents
- **Towera Translate** — Parallel translation datasets (English↔Yoruba, English↔Igbo, English↔Hausa, Yoruba↔Igbo, etc.)
- **Towera Studio** — Web platform for Nigerian contributors to record audio, translate sentences, validate recordings, upload folk stories, annotate data
- **Towera API** — Licensed access to speech, translation, OCR, handwriting, and pronunciation datasets

**Target Users:** AI/ML companies, research institutions, Nigerian universities, Nigerian technology startups, language preservation organizations
